## Week 13 — Unpredictability Index and Abstention

Combined winner prediction, calibrated probability, Fragility Score, error-risk probability, and model disagreement into the Against All Odds Unpredictability Index. Games were classified as Predict, Caution, or Abstain. The main experiment compared accuracy at 100%, 90%, 80%, 70%, and 60% coverage by removing the highest-Unpredictability games first. Risk-coverage, unpredictability-distribution, and accuracy-vs-coverage figures were created for the NBA, NFL, and Premier League.
